# Evidence protocol
Existing registry and September 2026 labels are published claims, not independently reproduced tests in this release.
Collect platform, surface, canvas, device, OS, exact app build, date, account state, caption/CTA state, screenshot reference, coordinate method, bounds and limitations. Distinguish observations from recommended margins.
Official claims need current primary URLs and access dates; observed UI needs actual observations. Do not fabricate missing context. Qualify unsupported claims through a reviewed content change. Redact personal information in evidence and agree private storage.
Review visible copy, schema, registry and downloads together. Automated margin checks cover the homepage table and three registry records; they do not validate physical devices or all canvas coordinates.
Flag records older than 90 days; do not automatically refresh review dates.
