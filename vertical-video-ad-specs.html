Understanding Paid Video Ad Overlays

For performance marketers and media buyers, creative placement directly affects campaign clarity. Commercial vertical ads carry greater screen obstruction than organic videos due to interactive CTA banners ('Shop Now', 'Learn More') and legal disclosure pills occupying the lower third of the viewport.

Recommended Guidelines for Paid Video Ads

1. The Hook Safe Region

Place primary hook statements and product offer callouts between Y: 220px and Y: 600px. This sits above the viewer's natural thumb-rest position and clears all top and bottom ad overlays.

2. Avoid Low Captions

Avoid placing subtitles below Y: 1450px. When TikTok or Meta renders an interactive CTA button, low captions create visual overlap that can obscure key product claims and mandatory disclaimers.

3. Social Proof Placement

Place review star ratings, press badges, and trust icons in the upper-left quadrant (X: 60px to 450px, Y: 180px to 380px) where eye-tracking patterns typically focus first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will TikTok or Meta reject my ad if text is in the danger zone?

While an ad may technically pass automated review, creative elements that overlap interactive CTA buttons can reduce readability. Keeping text inside the recommended safe box helps keep important visual information clear.

What resolution should I export for TikTok and Reels ads?

Export at 1080 × 1920 pixels at 30 or 60 frames per second with H.264 / MP4 encoding. Keep file sizes within platform upload limits (typically under 50MB for TikTok and under 250MB for Meta Ads) to maintain visual fidelity.
