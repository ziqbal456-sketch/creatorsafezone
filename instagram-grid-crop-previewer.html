Plan a crop without assuming Instagram has one fixed layout

This tool compares geometric center crops of a 1080 × 1920 canvas. The scenarios are not verified current Instagram feed or profile specifications. Presentation and cover controls can vary; inspect the destination app before publishing.

What each scenario removes

ScenarioRemaining areaTop and bottom removed
9:16 full frame1080 × 19200 px each
4:5 center crop1080 × 1350285 px each
1:1 center crop1080 × 1080420 px each

These numbers follow from canvas geometry: (1920 − remaining height) ÷ 2. They are not measurements of an Instagram screenshot.

Check a cover step by step

  1. Choose your image or video and select the crop scenario above.
  2. Mark the complete headline, face or logo. Areas outside the crop may be lost in that scenario.
  3. Switch scenarios to compare the same markers. Use custom margins if your intended framing differs.
  4. Apply any changes in your editor, then inspect the actual cover preview in Instagram. A clear region here is not a promise of compatibility with every layout.

Can I adjust a cover after publishing?

Check the editing options available for that post in your account. This planner does not assume a particular Instagram menu or guarantee that a published cover can be changed.
