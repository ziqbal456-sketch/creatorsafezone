Editorial policy

Our EDITORIAL POLICY and Source Standards

Last updated September 2026. CreatorSafeZone publishes practical short-form video QA guidance. Accuracy matters more than volume, and a useful qualification is never removed simply to make a claim sound stronger.

Evidence Labels

Official means a requirement or specification is explicitly published by the relevant platform. Observed means a UI behavior was measured in a stated test context. Recommended means CreatorSafeZone provides a practical working area. Mixed combines these sources and identifies the distinction.

Review and Corrections

Platform interfaces can change without notice. Pages should show a review date and the material test context when they present observed values. When a material error or outdated interface finding is identified, the relevant page and registry record should be corrected, dated, and reviewed. A visitor should not infer that an old measurement is a permanent platform guarantee.

Sources and Claims

Official platform sources are linked when a page discusses a documented requirement. Editorial guidance does not claim performance, reach, conversion, engagement, or approval outcomes unless a source and its specific context support that statement. Interface observations are not represented as official specifications.

Use of Ai

AI may assist research, outlining, quality review, or implementation. It does not replace source checking, direct testing, or editorial responsibility. Pages should be revised when new evidence changes the appropriate advice.
