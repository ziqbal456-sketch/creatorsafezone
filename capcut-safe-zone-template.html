How to Use the 9:16 Safe Zone Template in CapCut (Mobile & Desktop)

CapCut’s automated subtitle generator places captions across the lower third of your video by default. While this looks centered inside CapCut's editing canvas, once published on TikTok, Reels, or Shorts, the platform handle, follow button, and sound title obstruct the copy.

CapCut Mobile Setup (iOS & Android)

  1. Download the transparent PNG above to your camera roll.
  2. In CapCut, tap Overlay → Add Overlay and select the guide image.
  3. Pinch to scale it to 100% full screen.
  4. Adjust your automated captions upward so they sit entirely above the lower red boundary.
  5. Delete or hide the overlay layer before tapping Export.

CapCut Desktop Workflow

  1. Click Copy CapCut Guide above.
  2. Press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V directly into your CapCut Desktop timeline.
  3. Place the guide on the top video track above all edit layers.
  4. Set opacity to 60% and adjust text blocks accordingly.
  5. Hide or delete the track before rendering your MP4.
