Guide & Reference Notes

How to Use the 9:16 Safe Zone Template in CapCut (mobile & Desktop)

Captions placed near the bottom of an edit can overlap platform controls after publishing. Use the guide to plan their placement, then check the exported video in each destination app.

CapCut Mobile Setup (ios & Android)

  1. Download the transparent PNG above to your camera roll.
  2. In CapCut, tap Overlay → Add Overlay and select the guide image.
  3. Align all four guide edges with the project canvas. Check the project aspect ratio before scaling.
  4. Move the complete caption blocks inside your chosen working area. Check all boundaries and the subject, rather than moving every caption upward automatically.
  5. Delete or hide the overlay layer before tapping Export.

CapCut Desktop Workflow

  1. Click Export & use in editor → Copy guide to clipboard above.
  2. Press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V directly into your CapCut Desktop timeline.
  3. Place the guide on the top video track above all edit layers.
  4. Set opacity to 60% and adjust text blocks accordingly.
  5. Hide or delete the track before rendering your MP4.

Check the Exported Video, Not Just the Project

Remove the guide layer before export and review the longest caption in the delivered file. If subtitles are early or late, follow the subtitle timing guide. If another caption layer appears after upload, review burned-in and closed captions. Return to the video safe zone checker for your final placement review.
