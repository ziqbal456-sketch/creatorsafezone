Guide & Reference Notes

Keep Reels Captions Clear of the Controls

This tool checks the full-screen video layout against editable working margins. It does not simulate a profile cover crop or verify the current interface on every Instagram account.

Check the Complete Caption Block

  1. Choose your video and pause at the longest caption.
  2. Mark the whole block, including its background and the end of every line.
  3. Review the lower information area and right-side controls. The default bottom boundary is Y=1520 on our 1080 × 1920 canvas.
  4. Move or resize the real text in your editor, then check the exported version.

A Worked Reels Placement Example

A box at Y=1436 with a height of 78 ends at Y=1514. With the default bottom boundary at Y=1520, that leaves 6 px and produces Warning. Moving it to Y=1418 leaves 24 px at the bottom; other edges still need to pass. These numbers demonstrate the checker, not a measured Instagram layout.

When the Text Clears the Margin but Still Fails

Check whether a native caption layer overlaps your burned-in text, whether the longest line is wider than your marker, and whether the background makes the words hard to read. Start with captions hidden by app controls or caption readability.

Is the Problem the Cover Instead?

A cut-off cover headline needs a crop check. Use the Instagram crop planner and the cover text troubleshooting guide. Keep cover composition separate from full-screen caption placement.
