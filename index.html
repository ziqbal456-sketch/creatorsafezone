Recommended Social Media Safe Areas for Vertical Video (1080 × 1920)

1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16) is a common working canvas for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Native platform interface elements (like buttons, comment drawers, sound metadata, and account tags) can occupy a substantial portion of the frame, especially near the bottom and right side. The values below are conservative CreatorSafeZone working recommendations; their supporting UI capture archive is still pending.

Why Creative Placement Matters

When hook titles or subtitles overlap native interactive rails (such as like buttons or description drawers), visual friction increases. Obscured text can reduce immediate readability and make important messages harder to understand. Preserving clear margins helps keep the message clear.

Hardware Notches and Display Variances

Evaluating designs on desktop monitors often leads creators to overlook physical camera cutouts (such as Apple's Dynamic Island or Android hole-punch modules). A conservative top clearance helps reduce the risk of headings conflicting with hardware cutouts or platform UI.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vertical video safe area?

A vertical video safe area is the central viewport region of a 1080x1920 video that remains unobstructed by native platform UI buttons, usernames, comment drawers, sound titles, and smartphone hardware cutouts.

Are safe-zone measurements permanent platform specifications?

No. Platform interfaces vary by device, app version, font scaling, and ongoing A/B testing. Treat the displayed safe area as a practical working recommendation rather than a permanent guarantee. Learn more on our methodology page.
