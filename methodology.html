CALCULATIONS, SOURCES & LIMITS

How Our SAFE ZONE Calculations Work

Use this page to understand what a placement result means, where the working margins come from and what still needs independent verification.

A Safe Zone Is a Planning Boundary

A caption can be complete in an exported video and still be covered by app controls during playback. This checker represents potential obstructions with editable margins around a 1080 × 1920 planning canvas. The interface drawings help explain placement; they are not captured replicas of current app screens.

A cover crop is a separate geometric operation. The crop planner removes parts of the frame in the selected scenario. It does not establish which crop a particular Instagram account currently uses.

How the Checker Calculates a Result

You manually draw a rectangular region around an element. Its right edge is X + width and its bottom edge is Y + height. The checker compares all four edges with the selected working boundaries.

  • Safe: the region is at least 24 px inside every boundary.
  • Warning: it is inside the working area but closer than 24 px to an edge.
  • Covered: part of the region crosses a planning boundary.

Those labels describe the marked geometry at the moment you are checking. They do not assess unmarked text, caption accuracy, contrast, motion, audio, platform approval or every frame in the video.

The comparison walkthrough includes a reproducible example and explains how to hand the result to an editor. A suggested move changes your marker, not your uploaded video. An oversized region may need resizing before it can fit.

How a Working Estimate Becomes an Observation

We need a dated capture with the platform, viewing surface, device, OS, app build, account state, caption and CTA state, plus a coordinate method and limitations. Until that record exists, the estimate label stays. A single capture would describe that context, not every user's interface.

For your own final check, compare the exported video with the destination draft, note the obstruction, adjust your working margins and repeat. Return to the video placement hub.

Why Your Final App Preview Matters

Viewing surface, screen shape, caption length, account state and extra controls can change the space available. Check the longest caption, a moving scene and the end card in your actual destination. Keep a note of the context if you change the working margins.

Do not shrink all text to fit an assumed safe area. Review readability and the subject of the shot; a separate platform edit can be a better choice. Use the caption readability guide alongside placement checks.

Source Records and Corrections

The measurement registry retains the existing margin records and their evidence gaps. A new documentation review does not reset a measurement date or convert an estimate into an observation.
