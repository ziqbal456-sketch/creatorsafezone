CHECK → ADJUST → CONFIRM

Turn a warning into a better placement

  1. Mark what matters. Add boxes over your headline, subtitles, logo or CTA. Check representative frames where captions or graphics move.
  2. Compare all three results. A suggested move applies to one platform. After moving a box, review the other platforms again.
  3. Apply the change in your editor. Use the suggested direction and distance as a guide on a 1080 × 1920 canvas. Scale distances for other project sizes.
  4. Check the actual draft. Open the destination app's publishing preview. Captions, device settings and interface changes can alter what is covered.
