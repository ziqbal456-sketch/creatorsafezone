WORKED EXAMPLE · EDITORIAL UPDATE OCTOBER 8, 2026

Compare One Caption, Then Prepare the Editing Handoff

Compare the same creative against three working areas, mark the text that matters, then apply any placement changes in your editor. This checker uses recommendations and illustrative controls; it does not certify the current interface of any app.

1. Start with Your Media or the Local Sample

Select an image or MP4/WebM file, or choose Try a sample. A direct HTTPS video URL works only when its host allows browser playback and canvas access. Social watch-page links and live streams are not supported. Local files stay in your browser.

2. Mark the Whole Caption, Logo or Face

Choose Mark a region → Add region. Drag and resize the box around the complete element, or enter its coordinates under Exact position & size. For a video, pause at representative frames. The boxes do not track moving content or detect subtitles automatically.

3. Understand Why the Same Caption Gets Three Results

At the default margins, the sample subtitle is X: 90, Y: 1436, width: 740, height: 78 on a 1080 × 1920 canvas. Its bottom edge is therefore Y: 1514.

PreviewBottom working boundarySample subtitle result
TikTokY: 1500Covered: extends 14 px past the boundary
Instagram ReelsY: 1520Warning: only 6 px clearance
YouTube ShortsY: 1540Safe: 26 px clearance

Safe means at least 24 px inside all selected boundaries. Warning means inside but closer than 24 px to a boundary. Covered means the marker overlaps a planning margin. A green result checks marked geometry, not the entire video.

4. Find a Shared Placement, Then Edit the Original

With the sample subtitle selected, choose Find a shared placement. The tool moves it up 38 px to Y: 1398; its new bottom is Y: 1476. That gives 24 px clearance on TikTok, 44 px on Reels and 64 px on Shorts, so all three sample results become Safe.

For uploaded media, only the marker moves. Reposition the actual caption in CapCut, Canva or your editor, export the video and load that new file to check again. Custom margins, other sizes or other positions can produce a different suggestion. An oversized region may need resizing.

5. Take the Right Export into Your Editor

  • Copy guide / Download guide PNG: a transparent 1080 × 1920 overlay for the selected platform’s current margins, excluding your media.
  • Open template in Canva: opens the template; paste or upload your guide yourself. There is no automatic media transfer.
  • Download comparison PNG: a review image containing the displayed creative, illustrative UI and visible markers. It is not a final video.
  • Download placement report: margins, region coordinates and suggestions without your filename or source URL.

Before Publishing

Remove the guide layer from your final export. Review other video moments and the destination app’s draft preview, including longer captions or different controls. Adjusting the example caption length changes the illustration only; it does not update the numeric margins.

Continue with the CapCut workflow, crop scenarios or evidence and limitations. Platform-specific working ranges are explained in the TikTok, Reels and Shorts guides.

6. Decide Between One Master and Separate Edits

A shared placement helps when the same readable caption fits without covering the subject. If the shared area forces tiny text or hides an important action, prepare separate platform versions instead. Check the longest line and the end card in each version.

7. Give the Editor a Reproducible Brief

  • Name the moment: record the time in the video and the element to move.
  • Record the scenario: include the destination and any custom margins.
  • Export the evidence: use a comparison PNG to explain the issue and the placement report for exact coordinates. The PNG includes your displayed media; the report does not include its filename or source URL.
  • State the edit: specify the position or size change. Moving a marker here does not alter the original video.
  • Review the delivered file: remove the overlay, export and inspect each destination draft.

Example brief: “At the subtitle frame, move the marked 740 × 78 box from Y=1436 to Y=1398, keeping X=90. These coordinates use the default working estimates. Check that the revised caption stays readable and does not cover the subject.”

Use the CapCut guide, Canva overlay workflow or Premiere social overlay workflow for the editing step. If the media will not load, use the video playback troubleshooting guide.
