LEARN BY DOING

Why Can the Same Caption Pass One Preview and Fail Another?

Each platform preview has different working boundaries. A shared placement must clear all of them, which may require moving or resizing the caption. The sample lets you try a suggested move and undo it.

05 / CHECK THE DELIVERED FILE

Before You Publish

  1. Check representative moments. Review the hook, longest caption, moving shot and end card. Manually marked boxes do not follow moving objects.
  2. Read at phone size. Watch the whole line against the moving background and check timing with sound off.
  3. Inspect the actual destination. Check each platform, the cover and any paid placement separately. Review native caption layers too.
  4. Remove the guide and export. Play the delivered file, then load it back into the checker to review the version you will publish.

Selecting a local file does not upload it to CreatorSafeZone. Loading a direct video URL contacts that host. How media and site requests are handled.

06 / SOURCES & LIMITATIONS

Know What the Preview Can Tell You

The checker calculates overlap between the regions you mark and the selected boundaries. It does not detect captions, edit your media, test every device or certify a platform layout. The interface drawings are illustrative.

QUICK ANSWERS

Using the Safe Zone Checker

Does This Move the Text in My Video?

No. You draw the boxes, and a suggestion moves a box. Apply that change to the caption or logo in your editor, then check a new export.

Can I Download an Overlay Without Uploading a Video?

Yes. Open the export menu and download a transparent guide PNG using the selected margins. It excludes the sample and your media.

Should I Use One Safe Zone for Every Destination?

A shared working area is a useful starting point. If it compromises the subject or readability, make separate edits and check each destination preview.

What If the Preview Says Safe but the App Covers My Text?

Record which controls overlap your caption and adjust the working margins. Safe describes the chosen geometry, not every app interface. Follow the caption troubleshooting steps.

Browse all video editing guides →

Can I Reuse a 2023 or 2025 Safe Zone Template?

Use an older overlay only as a starting reference. Compare it with the current destination preview, adjust the planning margins and check the final file. A newer year in a filename does not prove that the boundaries were measured. See the current source review and its limits.
