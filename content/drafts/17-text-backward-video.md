# TEXT BACKWARD in Video? Fix the Right Layer

If lettering is mirrored in the saved video, apply a horizontal flip to the affected footage. First compare the original file, editor preview and export: a mirrored self-view does not establish that the recording is wrong. If only a caption or logo is backward, correct that layer instead of flipping the entire composition.

Keep the original recording and an editable project copy. You can usually investigate this with the player and editor you already use; choosing another app is not the first step.

## Find Where the Reversal Starts

Pause on a word whose correct appearance you know, such as a label held up to the camera. Inspect that moment in the saved source file, the editing project and a short export. Use the same word each time so you are comparing the picture rather than how familiar your face looks.

For example, [Zoom's settings documentation](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0060612) says its “Mirror my video preview” option affects how you see your own video. That is a specific preview setting, not evidence that every camera or recording app saves a mirrored file. Check the actual recording before trying to repair it.

If the source reads correctly but the project does not, inspect the clip's transform settings and any effect on its group or containing sequence. If the project reads correctly but the export does not, confirm the file and revision you opened, then inspect the render setup. Avoid adding another flip at each stage just to make a particular preview look familiar.

## Match the Fix to the Layer

Identify which parts of the composition are reversed before selecting anything in the editor:

| What You See | What to Inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Camera lettering reversed; added captions correct | The camera clip's flip or mirror setting |
| Camera lettering correct; one title reversed | The title, its group or its containing sequence |
| Everything reversed in one finished file | The original project's footage and text layers |

For a hypothetical whiteboard lesson, the board writing might be mirrored while newly added captions read normally. Flipping the completed frame would repair the board but reverse those captions. Apply the correction to the camera clip while leaving the readable caption layer alone.

When footage and captions have already been combined into one picture, you cannot select them as independent layers in that file. Return to the editable project or a clean recording where possible. The [caption-layer guide](https://creatorsafezone.com/guides/burned-in-vs-closed-captions) explains how to distinguish permanent text from a separate caption track.

## Apply a Horizontal Flip and Check the Meaning

Select the affected clip and find the editor's horizontal flip or mirror control. Microsoft's [Clipchamp flip instructions](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/clipchamp/how-to-flip-videos-vertically-or-horizontally) describe selecting the timeline asset, opening the stage toolbar's three-dot menu, then choosing Flip and the horizontal option. Its illustrated steps target personal accounts; the document says the same principles apply to work and school accounts. Other editors may use different labels.

After the change, inspect every visible word and logo in that shot. Also check arrows, hand gestures and spoken directions such as “on your left.” Making a sign readable does not by itself confirm that the demonstration still matches its narration.

Rotation turns a picture; it does not undo mirror writing. If the scene is sideways as well, handle orientation deliberately and check the lettering again. Use the [sideways-video guide](https://creatorsafezone.com/guides/video-plays-sideways-rotation) for that separate problem.

## Verify the Export Before Checking Placement

Render a short section that includes the corrected shot, a caption and a transition to another clip. Open the exported file outside the editor. Check that the words read correctly, the next shot has not been flipped unintentionally, and the captions and narration still agree.

Then load that export into the [comparison checker](https://creatorsafezone.com/check-all-platforms) to review the placement of manually marked labels or captions. A horizontal flip can move important content to the opposite side, so repeat the placement check after the edit. CreatorSafeZone does not flip or re-encode your video, read the words, or verify directional instructions. Confirm the final destination preview after making corrections in your editor.
