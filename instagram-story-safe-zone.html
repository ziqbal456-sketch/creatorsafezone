Story text and sticker placement: a working scenario

The default reserves 250 px at the top and 250 px at the bottom of a 1080 × 1920 canvas, leaving 1080 × 1420 px between them. These are CreatorSafeZone planning buffers, not official Instagram specifications or independently verified UI measurements.

The example header and reply tray are illustrations. Actual controls may differ. Mark your text or sticker, adjust the margins to your intended layout, and review a draft in Instagram before publishing.

How to check a Story

  1. Select your creative and mark each important text or sticker area.
  2. Review the status and use a suggested move if the marker overlaps a planning margin.
  3. For video, pause at several points. Markers remain fixed and do not track moving content.
  4. Apply the changes in your editor. Confirm readability and comfortable interaction on the destination device.

No particular coordinate guarantees easy tapping for everyone. Use the methodology to understand the evidence limits, or compare other formats with the cross-platform workflow.
