TikTok Safe Area and Overlay Templates

This checker uses a 1080 × 1920 canvas. At the current defaults, its planning area is 900 × 1340 px: 160 px from the top, 180 px from the right and 420 px from the bottom, with no added left margin. These are CreatorSafeZone estimates, not a measured or official TikTok specification.

Recommended TikTok Working Safe Area for 1080 × 1920 Video

This checker uses a 1080 × 1920 pixel canvas (a 9:16 aspect ratio). TikTok interface elements (such as the right-side interaction rail, account details, and bottom caption drawer) occupy a substantial portion of the mobile display.

The values below are conservative CreatorSafeZone working margins designed to reduce overlap risk. The underlying UI capture reference is still pending, so these values should not be presented as independently verified native-app measurements.

Recommended TikTok Working Margins (1080 × 1920 Canvas)

Recommended buffer for search icon, navigation tabs, and hardware camera cutouts.

Right Action Rail 160 – 180 px

Recommended buffer for creator avatar, likes, comments, bookmarks, and audio disc.

Recommended buffer for account handle, text captions, hashtags, and audio title.

Designed to provide a conservative working area across common mobile layouts; actual UI may vary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended TikTok safe zone for 1080 × 1920 video?

For a standard 1080×1920 vertical video, CreatorSafeZone recommends leaving approximately 140–160px clear at the top, 160–180px clear along the right margin, and 380–420px clear at the bottom. This leaves a central recommended working area of approximately 900×1340px at the upper-range defaults. Because native UI varies across devices and app builds, treat this as a practical working guideline rather than an official guarantee.

Why are my TikTok subtitles covered by the description?

TikTok reserves the lower portion of the screen for account handles, sound titles, and description text. In this checker’s default scenario, the bottom working boundary is Y: 1500. A region extending below it overlaps the planning margin; real caption placement still needs an in-app check.
