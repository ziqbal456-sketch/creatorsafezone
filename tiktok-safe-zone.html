Recommended TikTok Working Safe Area for 1080 × 1920 Video

While standard vertical video is exported at 1080 × 1920 pixels (a 9:16 aspect ratio), native TikTok interface elements (such as the right-side interaction rail, account details, and bottom caption drawer) occupy a substantial portion of the mobile display.

The values below are conservative CreatorSafeZone working margins designed to reduce overlap risk. The underlying UI capture reference is still pending, so these values should not be presented as independently verified native-app measurements.

Observed TikTok Working Margins (1080 × 1920 Canvas)

Top Clearance 140 – 160 px

Observed buffer for search icon, navigation tabs, and hardware camera cutouts.

Right Action Rail 160 – 180 px

Observed buffer for creator avatar, likes, comments, bookmarks, and audio disc.

Bottom Caption Drawer 380 – 420 px

Observed buffer for account handle, text captions, hashtags, and audio title.

Recommended Safe Area 900 × 1340 px

Designed to provide a conservative working area across common mobile layouts; actual UI may vary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended TikTok safe zone for 1080 × 1920 video?

For a standard 1080×1920 vertical video, CreatorSafeZone recommends leaving approximately 140–160px clear at the top, 160–180px clear along the right margin, and 380–420px clear at the bottom. This leaves a central recommended working area of approximately 900×1340px. Because native UI varies across devices and app builds, treat this as a practical working guideline rather than an official guarantee.

Why are my TikTok subtitles covered by the description?

TikTok reserves the lower portion of the screen for account handles, sound titles, and description text. Subtitles placed below Y-coordinate 1500 will frequently be covered by TikTok's caption drawer depending on the length of your caption.
