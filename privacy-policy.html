Privacy Policy & Client-Side Architecture

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 2.0

1. Zero Server Uploads (100% Client-Side Processing)

At CreatorSafeZone.com, we prioritize creator privacy and data confidentiality. All tools, video scrubbing players, overlay coordinate engines, and drag-and-drop simulators operate entirely inside your local web browser memory using the HTML5 File API and Canvas API.

Files you select from your device are processed locally and are not uploaded to CreatorSafeZone or a third-party processing service. Clearing the preview releases the selected media from the tool. Reports and comparison images are downloaded to your device when you request them.

2. Tracking Cookies and Personal Data

CreatorSafeZone does not utilize invasive profiling cookies, persistent fingerprinting identifiers, or user tracking scripts. We do not require user account registration, passwords, or personal credentials.

3. External Links & Design Platforms

Our service provides outbound integration links to third-party tools (such as Canva templates). CreatorSafeZone is not responsible for the privacy practices, content policies, or terms of third-party platforms once you navigate outside our domain.

The single-tool pages and cross-platform checker optionally load a direct HTTPS video URL that you provide. Your browser contacts that hosting service, which receives your IP address and the requested URL under its own privacy policy. The checker uses anonymous cross-origin media access, sends no page referrer, and does not send the URL or video to a CreatorSafeZone proxy. Source URLs are not included in downloaded reports. Local files and the built-in sample do not use this remote loading option.

4. Technical Inquiries

For technical inquiries regarding our client-side architecture or security protocols, contact our engineering team at privacy@creatorsafezone.com.
