Recommended YouTube Shorts Working Safe Area (1080 × 1920 Base)

While YouTube Shorts files are exported at standard 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio), the mobile player projects interactive overlays across the screen: the prominent white Subscribe button, the five-button action rail, and the lower title drawer.

The values below are conservative CreatorSafeZone working margins designed to reduce overlap risk. The underlying UI capture reference is still pending, so these values should not be presented as independently verified native-app measurements.

Observed YouTube Shorts Working Margins

Top Margin 120 – 140 px

Observed buffer for search glass, three-dot menu, and camera cutout.

Right Action Rail 140 – 160 px

Observed buffer for Like, Dislike, Comments, Share, and Remix sound button.

Bottom Channel Zone 340 – 380 px

Observed buffer for handle, Subscribe button, 2-line title, and audio track.

Recommended Safe Area 920 × 1400 px

Recommended working area designed to clear the Subscribe button, title drawer, and right action rail across common mobile layouts.

The Podcast Clipping Trap: Where to Place Split-Screen Speakers

Many creators repurpose long-form podcast interviews into stacked vertical videos (speaker 1 on top, speaker 2 on the bottom). When editing multi-cam vertical footage:

  • Top Speaker Framing: Keep the guest's head below Y: 180px to prevent the search icon or hardware notch from cutting off their forehead.
  • Bottom Speaker Framing: Position the host's chin and chest above Y: 1500px. Otherwise, the solid white Subscribe button will sit directly over their mouth.
  • Captions Sweet Spot: Place all animated captions centered between Y: 900px and Y: 1200px (the dividing line between both cameras).

DaVinci Resolve Setup

  1. Open Project Settings → Set Timeline Resolution to 1080 × 1920 (Vertical Resolution).
  2. Click Download Transparent Shorts PNG above.
  3. Drag the PNG into the Media Pool and place it on Video Track 3.
  4. Set Opacity to 60% and lock the track while positioning your titles.
  5. Mute or delete Track 3 prior to final delivery.

Adobe Premiere Pro Setup

  1. Click Copy Shorts Guide to Clipboard above.
  2. Switch to Premiere Pro and select your 9:16 Sequence.
  3. Select the top track and press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V.
  4. The overlay aligns to all 4 corners without manual scaling.
  5. Toggle track output (eyeball icon) to preview against native YouTube UI buttons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does YouTube Shorts cut off the right side of my captions?

The interaction rail contains five stacked buttons (Like, Dislike, Comments, Share, and Remix), taking up approximately 140px to 160px from the right boundary. Text extending past X: 920px will be partially or completely obscured.

Where should subtitles be placed on YouTube Shorts?

Subtitles are best centered between Y: 950px and Y: 1400px. Avoid placing subtitles in the bottom 360px where YouTube renders the channel handle, Subscribe button, and audio title.
