How much of a landscape video survives a vertical crop?

Filling a 1080 × 1920 canvas with a 1920 × 1080 frame keeps 31.64% of the original picture. Fitting the same clip preserves the full picture at about 1080 × 608, leaving about 656 pixels above and below. The calculator uses square pixels, keeps full precision during calculation and rounds displayed pixels to the nearest whole number.
